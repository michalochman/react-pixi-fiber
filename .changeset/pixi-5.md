Initial release. The PixiJS 5 adapter for PixiJS 5.x. It implements the 13 core tags and keeps `NineSlicePlane`, `SimpleMesh`, `SimplePlane` and `SimpleRope` as tags for `NineSliceSprite`, `MeshSimple`, `MeshPlane` and `MeshRope`. `Graphics` passes `geometry` to the constructor. It exports the `InteractionCompatibility`, `InteractionEventCompatibility`, `InteractiveComponent` and `PixiTypeFallback` types.
